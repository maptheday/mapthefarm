## What we do

Map The Farm puts drones to work on your property doing the jobs that are too slow on foot and too expensive from a plane.

**Field scouting** — fly your fields after rain to spot standing water, early weed pressure, or crop stress before it becomes a problem. Get images you can actually look at, not a dashboard someone else controls.

**Daily fence runs** — set a flight path along your fence lines and the drone checks them every morning. See the exact GPS location of any gap, fallen post, or downed wire before your cattle do.

You decide when it flies, what it looks at, and what happens with the data.

---

## How it's different

Most ag drone companies sell you a service where the drone, the software, and the data all belong to them. You pay per acre, per month, forever — and if something breaks, you wait.

We build and rent drones that you can actually own the relationship with:

- **Fix it yourself.** Every part is off-the-shelf and replaceable. No proprietary components that have to go back to the manufacturer.
- **Change what you want.** Don't like the flight path? Change it. Want to add a sensor? Add it. The hardware and software are open so the drone works for your operation, not a generic one.
- **Your data stays yours.** Images and flight logs live where you put them — not on our servers by default.
